# Brown Bear Technologies — Brand Guide

## Brand language

Technology for good.
Engineering for the real world.
Built for the messy middle.

## Current business focus

1. IPe — The Smart Connector
2. Aerospace Systems Engineering Services

## Website colors

Background: #050505
Primary text: #f4f0e8
Muted text: #8f887e

## Typography

Primary web font:
Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif

Headlines:
- Light/regular weight
- Large
- Tight letter spacing
- Minimalist

Body:
- Regular weight
- Muted warm gray/taupe
- Generous spacing

## Visual style

- Minimal
- Technical
- Premium but understated
- Black background
- Warm-white typography
- Lots of whitespace
- Fine divider lines
- No gradients
- No shadows
- No rounded cards
- No generic tech graphics

## Voice

Brown Bear should sound:
- practical
- experienced
- hands-on
- technically credible
- concise
- aerospace-focused

Avoid generic corporate language such as:
- innovative solutions
- cutting-edge
- world-class
- end-to-end transformation

IPe — The Smart Connector
